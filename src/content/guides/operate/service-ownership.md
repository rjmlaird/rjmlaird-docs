# Service ownership

Every maintained service should identify a responsible owner or role, a canonical repository, an operational contact route where appropriate, and a review date. If ownership is unknown, state `To confirm`; do not imply that a named individual or team has accepted responsibility.

## Minimum ownership record

- **Accountable owner:** role or team responsible for lifecycle and risk decisions.
- **Technical maintainer:** role responsible for code, configuration and deployment.
- **Documentation reviewer:** owner or delegate who confirms that the page remains accurate.
- **Review cadence:** normally six months for service records, and after material interface, access or deployment changes.
- **Evidence:** repository, deployment evidence or owner confirmation supporting lifecycle claims.

## Review triggers

Review the record when a hostname, owner, data flow, dependency, public interface, security boundary, deployment process or lifecycle changes. Update the canonical registry as part of the same change when it contains the affected fact.
