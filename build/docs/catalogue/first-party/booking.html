booking.rjmlaird.co.uk

Lifecycle: planned

This service is planned. It is documented from its intended scope and has not been verified as deployed in this repository. Do not treat it as a live interface until the catalogue says it is active.

Purpose​

booking.rjmlaird.co.uk is the boundary between visitors and the scheduling and payment providers. Its single responsibility is to let a visitor choose a service and be handed to the right provider. It never exposes customer records.

At a glance​

FieldValue

CatalogueGenerated public service catalogue

What it provides​

  • A list of bookable services
  • Booking intents, which hand a visitor to Cal.com or to checkout
  • Static confirmation pages for the provider redirects

Public interface​

Only the routes in HTTP contracts are public. The journey is in Customer journeys.

Boundaries​

This service does not:

  • Expose, list or search bookings, customers or payments
  • Take or store card data
  • Prove payment; only a verified webhook does, via hooks
  • Read from the CRM

Access model​

Public, with strict allow-lists, explicit CORS origins, validated request bodies and rate limiting. No stored data can be read through it.

Dependencies​

  • Cal.com for scheduling and Stripe for payment, through the providers' own flows
  • hooks for verified events
  • The service catalogue held in the private services repository

Operations​

  • Cal.com integration
  • Launch checklist
  • Deployment and environments
  • Security, privacy and access
  • Observability and status
  • Adding or changing a service

To confirm​

To confirm

The planning inventory recommended a path on the main site instead of a booking hostname. A dedicated hostname needs an ADR under ADR-0002.
